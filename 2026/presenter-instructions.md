This page outlines the guidelines and technical details for all presenters.
Please review them carefully to ensure your session runs smoothly and on time.

---

## Presentation Session

The date, time and location of your presentation slot can be found in the [online conference programme]({{site.baseurl}}/2026/programme). All talks take place in **NFT2**; posters, demos and sponsor stands are in the **Blue Room**.

---

## Presentation Formats

### Keynotes and Production Keynote

- **Duration:** 55 minutes, including audience questions

### Full Paper Sessions

- **Duration:** 15 minutes total
  - 12 minutes for presentation
  - 3 minutes for audience questions

### Industry Session

- **Duration:** 25 minutes total
  - 20 minutes for presentation
  - 5 minutes for audience questions

### Sponsor Talks

- **Duration:** 20 minutes, including audience questions

### Short Paper, Special Session and Demo Presenters

- **Spotlight Talk:** 1 minute to introduce your work, in the spotlight session on the day of your poster or demo
- **Posters and demos:** each poster or demo is shown on **one day only**, the day listed in the programme
- **Poster Size:** A0 portrait orientation (841 × 1189 mm)

---

## Slides and Laptops

### Full Paper Presenters

- Full paper talks are given from the **conference laptop** in NFT2
- Please send your slides to the Full Papers Chairs by **Friday 4 December**; they will email you the details
- PowerPoint (.pptx) is preferred; PDF is also accepted

### Keynote, Industry and Sponsor Speakers

- You may present from your **own laptop**, connected by **HDMI**
- If your laptop has no HDMI port, please bring your own adapter (for example USB-C to HDMI)

### Spotlight Talks

- Please send your spotlight slide as requested by the Short Papers Chairs in their email

---

## Audio-Visual Setup

### Equipment Provided in NFT2

- Lectern with microphone
- HDMI connection to the projector and screen
- Audio system for videos

### Important Requirements

**⚠️ Test Before Your Session**

- Please check your slides, and your laptop if you use your own, before your session, for example in the break before it
- NFT2 is available from 08:15 on Tuesday and from 09:00 on Wednesday

**⚠️ Run Presentations Locally**

- Please do not rely on the internet during your talk
- Embed videos in your presentation (H.264/MP4) rather than linking to online videos

---

## Presentation Guidelines

**File Formats:**

- 16:9 aspect ratio preferred
- Test any animations or transitions in advance

**Timing:**

- Practice your presentation to stay within time limits
- Session chairs will provide time warnings
- Respect the schedule to allow time for questions and following speakers

**Backup:**

- Bring a USB drive with your presentation as backup

---

## Poster and Demo Requirements

For Short Paper, Special Session and Demo presenters:

- **Size:** A0 portrait (841 × 1189 mm / 33.1 × 46.8 inches)
- **Mounting:** Poster boards and fixings will be provided in the Blue Room
- **Setup:** Please put up your poster before the first poster session on your day (the lunch break), and take it down at the end of that day
- **Attendance:** Please be at your poster or demo during the lunch break and the afternoon coffee break of your day (the morning coffee break is for sponsor demos)
- **Demos:** a table, power and Wi-Fi are provided; the Short Papers Chairs have your requirements
